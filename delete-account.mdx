This page applies to all apps published on Google Play by **ARAVIND CHOWDARY KAMANI (KAC07)**. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time, for any of our apps.

## Option 1: Delete from inside the app

1. Open the app and sign in.
2. Go to **Profile → Settings → Account**.
3. Tap **Delete Account**.
4. Confirm when prompted. Your account is deleted and you are signed out.

## Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed or cannot sign in, email us at [support@everoaistech.com](mailto:support@everoaistech.com?subject=Account%20Deletion%20Request) from the address registered to your account, with the subject **Account Deletion Request**. Please include the **name of the app** you want your account deleted from.

We verify requests by replying to the registered email address. Requests are processed within **[30] days**, and you will receive a confirmation when complete.

## What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, the following is permanently removed:

| Data type | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Account information (name, email, phone, profile photo) | Deleted |
| User-generated content (posts, uploads, messages, settings) | Deleted |
| Usage and device data linked to your account | Deleted or anonymized |
| Push notification tokens | Deleted |

## What data may be retained

| Data type | Why | Retention period |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transaction / billing records | Tax, accounting and legal obligations | [7 years] |
| Security and fraud-prevention logs | Protecting the service | [90 days] |
| Backups | Disaster recovery; overwritten on a rolling cycle | Up to [30 days] |

Retained data is kept only for the stated purpose and period, then permanently deleted.

## Partial deletion

To delete specific data without closing your account (for example, uploaded content or history), use **Settings → Privacy → Manage my data** in the app, or email us at [support@everoaistech.com](mailto:support@everoaistech.com).
